👤 2. Samee akoon2. Create an account

  1. Fur app-ka, riix Is-diiwaangeli.
  2. Dooro wadanka iyo magaalada aad joogto.
  3. Geli email-kaaga iyo eray sir ah (ugu yaraan 6 xaraf).
  4. Dooro 👤 Shakhsi (qof caadi ah) ama 🏢 Shirkad (ganacsi). Mar dambe waad beddeli kartaa.
🎁 Akoon cusub wuxuu helayaa 7 maalmood oo bilaash ah.

Ma ilowday erayga sirta ah?

Bogga gelitaanka riix "Ma ilowday erayga sirta ah?". Link ayaa email-kaaga ku imanaya, ku samee eray cusub.

  1. Open the app and tap Sign up.
  2. Choose your country and city.
  3. Enter your email and a password (at least 6 characters).
  4. Choose 👤 Personal or 🏢 Business. You can switch later.
🎁 New accounts get 7 days free.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page tap "Forgot your password?". A link is sent to your email to set a new one.

＋ 3. Ku dar dakhli ama kharash3. Add income or expense

  1. Hoosta riix ＋ Ku dar.
  2. Dooro + Dakhli (lacag soo gashay) ama − Kharash (lacag baxday).
  3. Dooro halka lacagtu ku jirto: EVC Plus, Bangi ama Kaash.
  4. Dooro qaybta (tusaale: Cunto, Kiro, Mushahar), geli lacagta, riix Kaydi.

Lacagta waxaad ku qori kartaa Dollar ama Shilin Soomaali (sare: "Nooca lacagta").

✏️ Tiro khaldan ma qortay? Liiska "Dhaqdhaqaaqa bishan" riix ✏️ Beddel, sax, kadib Kaydi beddelka.

🏠 Guriga

Halkan waxaad ku arkaysaa haraagaaga (EVC, Bangi, Kaash), kaydka bishan, iyo shaxda 6-dii bilood.

  1. Tap ＋ Add at the bottom.
  2. Choose + Income or − Expense.
  3. Choose where the money is: EVC Plus, Bank or Cash.
  4. Pick a category (e.g. Food, Rent, Salary), enter the amount, tap Save.

Amounts can be in Dollar or Somali shilling (top: "Currency").

✏️ Typed a wrong amount? In "This month's transactions" tap ✏️ Edit, fix it, then Save changes.

🏠 Home

See your balance (EVC, Bank, Cash), this month's savings and a 6-month chart.

📅 4. Qorshe: miisaaniyad, biilal, kayd4. Plan: budget, bills, savings

Hoosta riix 📅 Qorshe:

  • Miisaaniyadda bishii: dhig xadka kharashka. Digniin ayaad helaysaa marka aad u dhowaato.
  • Kharashyada joogtada ah: biilasha (kiro, koronto, biyo). Xusuusin ayaad helaysaa ka hor maalinta. Marka aad bixiso riix Bixi, haddii aad khaldantana ↩ Ka noqo.
  • Kaydka iyo buffer-ka: dhig yool kayd ah oo raac horumarkiisa.
  • Deymaha (Shirkad): lacagta lagugu leeyahay iyo tan aad ku leedahay.

Tap 📅 Plan at the bottom:

  • Monthly budget: set a spending limit and get a warning when you get close.
  • Recurring bills: rent, electricity, water, with a reminder before the due day. Tap Pay when paid, or ↩ Undo if by mistake.
  • Savings and buffer: set a savings goal and track it.
  • Debts (Business): money owed to you and money you owe.

📄 5. Warbixin, backup iyo shaqaale5. Reports, backup and staff

Hoosta riix 👤 Akoon:

  • Warbixin: dooro bisha ama sannadka, kadib ⬇ Excel (CSV) ama 🖨 PDF (dooro "Save as PDF").
  • Backup: ⬇ Soo deji backup si aad xogtaada u kaydiso. ⬆ Soo celi backup si aad dib ugu soo celiso.
  • Shaqaale (Shirkad): ku dar email-ka shaqaalaha si ay isla akoonka shirkadda u isticmaalaan.
  • Luqadda: Soomaali ama English.
  • 🚪 Ka bax: sare midig ama Akoon hoostiisa.

Tap 👤 Account at the bottom:

  • Reports: pick month or year, then ⬇ Excel (CSV) or 🖨 PDF (choose "Save as PDF").
  • Backup: ⬇ Download backup to keep a copy; ⬆ Restore backup to bring it back.
  • Staff (Business): add staff emails so they share the company account.
  • Language: Somali or English.
  • 🚪 Sign out: top right, or under Account.
