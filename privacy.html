Xogta aan kaydinno

  • Akoonka: emailkaaga iyo erayga sirta ah (si ammaan ah ayaa loo kaydiyaa, annaguna ma arki karno).
  • Xogta dhaqaalaha: dakhliga, kharashyada, kaydka iyo yoolalka aad adigu gelisay.
  • Meesha aad joogto: magaalada aad dooratay iyo wadanka (laga ogaado saacadda taleefanka), si aan u ogaanno meelaha HantiGo laga isticmaalo. Meel sax ah (GPS) ma qaadno.
  • Lambarka EVC: haddii aad gelisid, si loo xaqiijiyo lacag-bixintaada.

Sida aan u isticmaalno

Xogtaada waxaa loo isticmaalaa oo keliya si app-ku kuugu shaqeeyo, iyo si loo xaqiijiyo subscription-kaaga. Xogtaada ma iibinno, lalamana wadaagno cid saddexaad, xayeysiisna uma isticmaalno. Haddii aad akoonka shirkaddaada ku darto shaqaale, waxay arkayaan qaybta Shirkadda oo keliya.

Halka ay ku kaydsan tahay

Xogta waxay ku kaydsan tahay server-ka Supabase (Midowga Yurub), nuqul ayaana ku jira taleefankaaga si app-ku internet la'aan u shaqeeyo.

Tirtirista xogta

Gudaha app-ka: Warbixin iyo backup → Tirtir akoonkayga. Akoonka iyo xogta oo dhan isla markiiba waa la tirtirayaa. Haddii aadan geli karin, nala soo xiriir: asad.moallim@gmail.com, 30 maalmood gudahood ayaan tirtiraynaa.

HantiGo – Privacy Policy

Data we store: your email address and password (stored securely, we cannot read it), the income, expenses, savings and goals you enter, and the EVC number you choose to add for payment verification, and the city you choose plus your country (from your phone's time zone) so we know where HantiGo is used. We never collect precise (GPS) location.

How we use it: only to run the app for you and to verify your subscription. We do not sell or share your data with third parties or use it for advertising. If you add staff to your company account, they only see the Business section.

Where it is stored: on Supabase servers in the European Union, with a copy on your device so the app works offline.

Deleting your data: in the app, open Reports and backup → Delete my account; your account and all data are deleted immediately. If you cannot sign in, email asad.moallim@gmail.com and we delete it within 30 days.
