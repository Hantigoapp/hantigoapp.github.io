1. Cidda aan nahay

HantiGo waa app lagu maareeyo lacagta, mid shakhsi ah (Shakhsi) iyo mid shirkad ah (Shirkad). Waxaa aasaasay oo maamula Asad Moallim. Siyaasaddani waxay khusaysaa dhammaan meelaha HantiGo laga isticmaalo: website-ka hantigoapp.github.io, app-ka taleefanka ama kombiyuutarka lagu rakibo, iyo app-ka Android (APK iyo Uptodown). Dhammaantood waa isku xog iyo isku nidaam.

2. Xogta aan kaydinno

Akoonkaaga

  • Emailkaaga iyo erayga sirta ah. Erayga sirta ah waxaa loo kaydiyaa qaab qarsoon (hash), annaguna ma arki karno.
  • Nooca akoonka (Shakhsi ama Shirkad), qorshaha ($3 ama $7 bishii), taariikhda aad is diiwaangelisay, maalmaha tijaabada, ilaa goorta lacagtu kuu furan tahay, iyo goorta ugu dambeysay ee aad gashay.
  • Wadanka iyo magaalada aad dooratay. Wadanka waxaa sidoo kale laga qiyaasaa saacadda (time zone) taleefankaaga.

Xogta dhaqaalaha ee aad adigu geliso

  • Lacagta soo gashay iyo tan ka baxday, qaybaha, liisaska, miisaaniyadda, biilasha, kaydka iyo yoolalka, deymaha iyo xusuusinta.
  • Magacaaga haddii aad ku qorto si uu warbixinta uga soo baxo.

Akoonnada Shirkadda (haddii aad isticmaasho)

  • Magaca shirkadda iyo astaanta (logo) haddii aad ku darto.
  • Emailka shaqaalaha aad ku darto, doorkooda (tusaale "Macallin") iyo mushaharkooda.
  • Codsiyada fasaxa iyo warbixinnada xanuunka ee shaqaaluhu soo diraan.
  • Rasiidyada iyo qaansheegyada, iyo xogta macmiilka aad ku qorto (magac, taleefan, email).

Lacag-bixinta

  • Lambarka aad lacagta ka dirtay, lambarka xawilaadda (transaction), habka (EVC Plus ama WaafiPay), iyo sawirka fariinta haddii aad doorato inaad soo dirto.

Waxa taleefankaaga ku kaydsan (ma aha server-ka)

  • Luuqadda aad dooratay, nuqul xogtaada ah si app-ku u furmo internet la'aan, iyo isbeddellada sugaya in la diro.
  • Haddii aad shido quful PIN ah: PIN-ka waxaa loo kaydiyaa qaab qarsoon (hash) taleefankaaga oo keliya, server-kana looma diro.

3. Waxa aanan ururin

  • Meel sax ah (GPS), liiska dadka (contacts), fariimahaaga SMS, ama sawirradaada. Kaliya sawirka ama faylka aad adigu doorato ayaa la isticmaalaa.
  • Fariinta EVC ee aad ku dhejiso foomka lacag-bixinta waxaa lagu akhriyaa taleefankaaga si foomka loo buuxiyo. Qoraalka fariinta oo dhan lama diro, lamana kaydiyo.
  • Aqoonsiga xayeysiiska, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel ama aalad kale oo raadraac ah. HantiGo ma laha.
  • Kaarka bangiga ama PIN-ka EVC Plus.

4. Sida aan u isticmaalno xogtaada

  • Si app-ku kuugu shaqeeyo, xogtaaduna ugu dhex socoto taleefankaaga, kombiyuutarkaaga iyo shaqaalahaaga.
  • Si loo xaqiijiyo lacag-bixintaada loona furo akoonkaaga.
  • Si laguugu diro rasiidka lacagta, emailka dib u dejinta erayga sirta ah, iyo rasiidyada Shirkadda.
  • Si aan kaaga jawaabno marka aad nala soo xiriirto.
  • Tirakoob guud (tusaale: imisa qof ayaa ka isticmaala magaalo kasta), si aan HantiGo u horumarinno.

Xogtaada ma iibinno, ma kirayno, xayeysiisna uma isticmaalno.

5. Cidda arki karta xogtaada

  • Adiga oo keliya ayaa arka xogtaada Shakhsi. Server-ka (Row Level Security) wuxuu diidaa qof kale oo isku daya inuu akhriyo.
  • Shaqaalaha aad ku darto (Shirkad): waxay arkaan qaybta Shirkadda oo keliya, ma arkaan xogtaada Shakhsi. Marka aad ka saarto, isla markiiba way ka go'aan.
  • Maamulka HantiGo: bogga maamulka wuxuu muujiyaa oo keliya emailka, lambarka lacag-bixinta, wadanka iyo magaalada, qorshaha, taariikhaha tijaabada iyo lacagta, codsiyada lacagta, magaca shirkadda, goorta ugu dambeysay ee aad gashay, iyo tirada waxyaabaha aad gelisay. Ma muujiyo dakhligaaga iyo kharashkaaga, mana eegno.
  • Adeegyada naga caawiya socodsiinta (waxay xogta u isticmaalaan oo keliya inay adeegga bixiyaan):
    • Supabase: kaydka xogta, gelitaanka iyo shaqooyinka server-ka.
    • Google (Gmail): dirista emailada.
    • GitHub Pages: martigelinta website-ka (waxay arkaan cinwaanka IP-ga ee booqdaha).
    • Google Fonts: farta qoraalka ee bogga hore (Google wuxuu arkaa cinwaanka IP-ga).
  • WhatsApp: kaliya haddii aad riixdo badhanka WhatsApp. Markaas siyaasadda WhatsApp ayaa khusaysa.
  • Sharciga: kaliya haddii sharci nagu qasbo.

6. Halka ay ku kaydsan tahay iyo internet la'aan

Xogtaada waxay ku kaydsan tahay server-ada Supabase. Nuqul ayaa ku jira taleefankaaga si app-ku u shaqeeyo xitaa internet la'aan. Waxa aad gelisid internet la'aan way sugayaan, marka internetku soo laabtana si toos ah ayaa loo diraa. Haddii laba qalab isku shay beddelaan, isbeddelka ugu dambeeyay ayaa guuleysta. Haddii aad ka baxdo adigoo isbeddello sugaya, app-ku wuu kuu digayaa.

Haddii aad wadaagto taleefan ama kombiyuutar, ka bax (Ka bax) markaad dhammayso, ama shid quful PIN ah.

7. Lacag-bixinta

  • Lacagta waxaad ku bixisaa app-ka EVC Plus ama WaafiPay, una dirtaa +252 61 373 5086. HantiGo ma arko, mana weydiiyo PIN-kaaga.
  • Kadib waxaad soo dirtaa lambarka aad ka dirtay iyo lambarka xawilaadda, iyo haddii aad rabto sawirka fariinta, si aan u xaqiijinno.
  • Marka la ansixiyo, sawirka fariinta waa la tirtiraa, waxaana emailkaaga laguugu soo diraa rasiid PDF ah (nuqul ayaa maamulka u yimaada).
  • Qiimaha: Shakhsi $3 bishii, Shirkad $7 bishii. Kaarka bangiga ma kaydinno.

8. Emailada

Waxaan kuu dirnaa oo keliya emailada lagama maarmaanka ah: dib u dejinta erayga sirta ah, rasiidka lacag-bixinta, iyo rasiidyada iyo qaansheegyada Shirkadda (macmiilka ayaa loo diraa, nuqulna adiga). Waxaa lagu diraa Gmail, cinwaanka HantiGo. Ma dirno email xayeysiis ah.

9. Amniga

  • Xiriir kasta waa qarsoon yahay (HTTPS).
  • Erayga sirta ah waxaa loo kaydiyaa hash, qofna ma akhrin karo.
  • Row Level Security: server-ka ayaa hubiya in qof kasta xogtiisa oo keliya uu akhrin karo ama beddeli karo.
  • Shaqaaluhu waxay xogta shirkadda ku galaan oo keliya hawlo server-ka ku jira oo hubiya inuu milkiiluhu ku daray.
  • Supabase wuxuu xogta ku kaydiyaa qaab qarsoon (encryption at rest).
  • Quful PIN ah oo ikhtiyaari ah, iyo digniin ka hor ka bixitaanka haddii isbeddello sugayaan.
  • Furaha Gmail iyo furayaasha kale ee sirta ah waxay ku jiraan server-ka, app-ka kuma jiraan.

Nidaam internet ah oo 100% ammaan ah ma jiro. Haddii dhacdo amni oo xogtaada saameysa, waan kuu sheegi doonnaa sida ugu dhakhsaha badan.

10. Muddada aan haynno iyo tirtirista

  • Xogtaada waan haynaa inta akoonkaagu jiro. Haddii tijaabadu dhammaato, xogtu way kuu kaydsan tahay si aad u sii wadato markaad bixiso.
  • Tirtir akoonkayga: gudaha app-ka, fur Akoon → Warbixin iyo backup → Tirtir akoonkayga. Akoonka, xogta dhaqaalaha, liiska shaqaalaha iyo xog kasta oo kugu xiran isla markiiba waa la tirtiraa. Sidoo kale waa lagaa saaraa liisaska shaqaalaha shirkado kale.
  • Waxa sii jiri kara: emailada horay loo diray (rasiidyada) oo ku jira sanduuqyada emailka, nuqullada kaydka ee Supabase oo si toos ah u baaba'a muddo kooban kadib, iyo nuqulka taleefankaaga ilaa aad ka baxdo ama aad tirtirto xogta browser-ka.
  • Haddii aadan geli karin, nagu soo qor asad.moallim@gmail.com. 30 maalmood gudahood ayaan tirtiraynaa.

11. Xuquuqdaada

  • Inaad saxdo: shay kasta waad beddeli kartaa (✏️ Beddel), macluumaadka akoonkana Akoon.
  • Inaad tirtirto: shay kasta ama akoonka oo dhan.
  • Inaad su'aal ama cabasho soo dirto: 30 maalmood gudahood ayaan kaaga jawaabnaa.

  • 📍No GPS. Only the country and city you choose.

1. Who we are

HantiGo is a money management app for personal use (Personal) and for companies (Business). It was founded and is run by Asad Moallim. This policy covers every place HantiGo is used: the website hantigoapp.github.io, the app installed on a phone or computer, and the Android app (APK and Uptodown). They all share the same data and the same system.

2. Data we store

Your account

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored as a hash; we cannot read it.
  • Account type (Personal or Business), plan ($3 or $7 per month), sign-up date, trial days, paid-until date and when you last signed in.
  • The country and city you choose. The country is also estimated from your phone's time zone.

Financial data you enter yourself

  • Income and spending, categories, lists, budgets, bills, savings and goals, debts and reminders.
  • Your name, if you enter it so it appears on reports.

Business accounts (if you use them)

  • Company name and logo, if you add them.
  • The email addresses of staff you add, their role (for example "Teacher") and salary.
  • Leave requests and sick reports sent by staff.
  • Receipts and invoices, and the customer details you type in (name, phone, email).

Payments

  • The number you paid from, the transaction number, the method (EVC Plus or WaafiPay), and a screenshot of the payment message if you choose to send one.

Stored on your device (not on the server)

  • Your chosen language, a copy of your data so the app opens offline, and changes waiting to be sent.
  • If you turn on a PIN lock: the PIN is stored as a hash on your device only and is never sent to the server.

3. What we do not collect

  • Precise location (GPS), contacts, your SMS messages or your photos. Only the picture or file you pick yourself is used.
  • An EVC message you paste into the payment form is read on your device to fill in the form. The full message text is not sent or stored.
  • Advertising IDs, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or any other tracking tool. HantiGo has none.
  • Bank cards or your EVC Plus PIN.

4. How we use your data

  • To run the app for you and keep your data in sync between your phone, computer and staff.
  • To verify your payment and open your account.
  • To send you payment receipts, password reset emails and Business receipts.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • For overall statistics (for example how many people use HantiGo in each city) to improve the app.

We do not sell, do not rent and do not use your data for advertising.

5. Who can see your data

  • Only you can see your Personal data. The server (Row Level Security) refuses anyone else who tries to read it.
  • Staff you add (Business): they see only the Business section, never your Personal data. When you remove them, their access ends at once.
  • HantiGo administration: the admin page shows only your email, payment number, country and city, plan, trial and payment dates, payment requests, company name, last sign-in, and how many entries you have. It does not show your income or spending, and we do not look at them.
  • Services that help us run HantiGo (they use data only to provide their service):
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions.
    • Google (Gmail): sending emails.
    • GitHub Pages: hosting the website (sees visitors' IP addresses).
    • Google Fonts: the font on the first page (Google sees the IP address).
  • WhatsApp: only if you tap the WhatsApp button. WhatsApp's own policy then applies.
  • The law: only if the law requires us.

6. Where it is stored and offline use

Your data is stored on Supabase servers. A copy is kept on your device so the app works without internet. Anything you enter offline waits and is sent automatically when the internet returns. If two devices change the same item, the latest change wins. If you sign out while changes are waiting, the app warns you.

If you share a phone or computer, sign out when you are done, or turn on the PIN lock.

7. Payments

  • You pay in the EVC Plus or WaafiPay app, to +252 61 373 5086. HantiGo never sees or asks for your PIN.
  • You then send the number you paid from and the transaction number, and a screenshot if you like, so we can verify it.
  • Once approved, the screenshot is deleted and a PDF receipt is emailed to you (a copy goes to the administration).
  • Prices: Personal $3 per month, Business $7 per month. We do not store bank cards.

8. Emails

We only send necessary emails: password reset, payment receipts, and Business receipts and invoices (sent to the customer, with a copy to you). They are sent through Gmail from the HantiGo address. We do not send marketing emails.

9. Security

  • Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as hashes that nobody can read.
  • Row Level Security: the server checks that each person can read or change only their own data.
  • Staff reach company data only through server functions that check the owner added them.
  • Supabase stores data encrypted at rest.
  • Optional PIN lock, and a warning before signing out if changes are waiting.
  • The Gmail key and other secret keys live on the server, not in the app.

No internet system is 100% secure. If a security incident affects your data, we will tell you as quickly as possible.

10. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data while your account exists. If your trial ends, your data stays saved so you can continue when you pay.
  • Delete my account: in the app, open Account → Reports and backup → Delete my account. Your account, financial data, staff list and everything linked to you are deleted immediately. You are also removed from other companies' staff lists.
  • What may remain: emails already sent (receipts) in mailboxes, Supabase backup copies that are deleted automatically after a short time, and the copy on your device until you sign out or clear the browser data.
  • If you cannot sign in, email asad.moallim@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.

11. Your rights

  • See and download: Excel, Word, PDF, and a full backup (JSON) that you can restore.
  • Correct: edit any entry (✏️ Edit), and your account details under Account.
  • Delete: any entry, or the whole account.
  • Ask a question or complain: we answer within 30 days.

12. Business accounts

If you enter your staff's or customers' data in HantiGo, you are responsible for telling them and having their permission. Staff and customers can ask you or us about it.

13. Children

HantiGo is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, use it with your parent's permission. If you learn that a child has opened an account, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes. We will tell you about important changes in the app or by email before they take effect.

1. من نحن

HantiGo تطبيق لإدارة المال للاستخدام الشخصي (شخصي) وللشركات (شركة). أسّسه ويديره أسعد معلم. تشمل هذه السياسة كل أماكن استخدام HantiGo: الموقع hantigoapp.github.io، والتطبيق المثبّت على الهاتف أو الحاسوب، وتطبيق أندرويد (APK وUptodown). وكلها تتشارك البيانات نفسها والنظام نفسه.

2. البيانات التي نحفظها

حسابك

  • بريدك الإلكتروني وكلمة المرور. تُحفظ كلمة المرور بشكل مُشفّر (hash) ولا نستطيع قراءتها.
  • نوع الحساب (شخصي أو شركة)، والخطة (3$ أو 7$ شهريًا)، وتاريخ التسجيل، وأيام التجربة، وتاريخ انتهاء الاشتراك، وآخر تسجيل دخول.
  • الدولة والمدينة اللتان تختارهما. وتُقدَّر الدولة أيضًا من المنطقة الزمنية لهاتفك.

البيانات المالية التي تُدخلها بنفسك

  • الدخل والمصروفات، والفئات، والقوائم، والميزانية، والفواتير، والادخار والأهداف، والديون والتذكيرات.
  • اسمك، إذا أدخلته ليظهر في التقارير.

حسابات الشركات (إن استخدمتها)

  • اسم الشركة وشعارها، إن أضفتهما.
  • البريد الإلكتروني للموظفين الذين تضيفهم، ودورهم (مثل "معلم") ورواتبهم.
  • طلبات الإجازة وبلاغات المرض التي يرسلها الموظفون.
  • الإيصالات والفواتير، وبيانات العميل التي تكتبها (الاسم، الهاتف، البريد).

الدفع

  • الرقم الذي دفعت منه، ورقم العملية، والطريقة (EVC Plus أو WaafiPay)، وصورة رسالة الدفع إن اخترت إرسالها.

ما يُحفظ على جهازك (وليس على الخادم)

  • اللغة التي اخترتها، ونسخة من بياناتك ليعمل التطبيق دون إنترنت، والتغييرات التي تنتظر الإرسال.
  • إذا فعّلت قفل الرمز (PIN): يُحفظ مُشفّرًا (hash) على جهازك فقط ولا يُرسل إلى الخادم.

3. ما لا نجمعه

  • الموقع الدقيق (GPS)، أو جهات الاتصال، أو رسائلك النصية، أو صورك. نستخدم فقط الصورة أو الملف الذي تختاره بنفسك.
  • رسالة EVC التي تلصقها في نموذج الدفع تُقرأ على جهازك لملء النموذج، ولا يُرسل نصها الكامل ولا يُحفظ.
  • معرّفات الإعلانات أو Google Analytics أو Facebook Pixel أو أي أداة تتبّع. HantiGo لا يحتوي على أي منها.
  • البطاقات البنكية أو رمز EVC Plus السري.

4. كيف نستخدم بياناتك

  • لتشغيل التطبيق لك ومزامنة بياناتك بين هاتفك وحاسوبك وموظفيك.
  • للتحقق من دفعتك وفتح حسابك.
  • لإرسال إيصالات الدفع، ورسائل إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور، وإيصالات الشركة.
